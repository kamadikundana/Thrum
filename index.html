        About Thrum

        Thrum is a camera-based heart rhythm screening aid built for the Congressional App Challenge. It measures pulse-rate variability (PRV) from your phone's camera and flash, and compares new readings against your own personal history rather than a fixed population threshold.

        Not a medical device. Always consult a doctor for diagnosis.
